# 🌍 AquaPercent AI

### Predictive Water Infrastructure Intelligence Platform

> **Predicting water failures before communities suffer.**

AquaPercent AI is an AI-powered predictive infrastructure intelligence platform designed to monitor, analyze, and forecast water system behavior in real time.

Instead of reacting to leaks and failures after damage occurs, AquaPercent AI predicts risks before they escalate — helping governments, utilities, industries, and farmers reduce water loss, optimize infrastructure, and protect livelihoods.

---

# 🚨 The Problem

Water infrastructure failures are often invisible until catastrophic damage occurs.

Across cities, villages, and agricultural regions:

* Hidden pipeline leaks waste millions of liters of water
* Pressure instability damages infrastructure
* Delayed maintenance increases operational costs
* Farmers suffer crop loss due to unnoticed irrigation failures
* Existing systems are reactive instead of predictive

One unnoticed pipeline leak caused nearly **₹60,000 in crop loss** on a farm connected to our team.

That moment inspired AquaPercent AI.

---

# 💡 The Solution

AquaPercent AI transforms traditional water infrastructure into an intelligent predictive ecosystem.

The platform continuously analyzes infrastructure behavior using:

* Real-time IoT monitoring
* Predictive analytics
* AI-based anomaly detection
* Infrastructure risk intelligence
* Smart alerting systems
* Operational decision intelligence

Instead of asking:

❌ “What failed?”

AquaPercent AI asks:

✅ “What is likely to fail next?”

# 🏗 System Architecture

AquaPercent AI is built as a scalable end-to-end infrastructure intelligence system.

## 🔹 Sensor Layer

IoT sensors collect:

* Water flow rate
* Pressure metrics
* Environmental conditions
* Pipeline behavior

## 🔹 Data Transmission Layer

Embedded systems securely transmit real-time telemetry to the cloud using lightweight communication protocols.

## 🔹 Cloud Intelligence Layer

Streaming infrastructure data is processed, stored, and analyzed in real time.

## 🔹 AI Prediction Layer

Machine learning models:
